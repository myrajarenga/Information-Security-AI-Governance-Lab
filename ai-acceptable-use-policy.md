# AI Acceptable Use Policy

## Purpose

This policy provides basic rules for the safe and responsible use of artificial intelligence tools at Mwangaza Research.

The purpose is to reduce the risk of sensitive organizational, personal, research, and confidential information being exposed through AI tools.

## Who This Applies To

This policy applies to:

- Employees
- Contractors
- Researchers
- Developers
- Temporary staff
- Anyone using organizational information with AI tools

## Approved Use

Employees may use approved AI tools for activities such as:

- Drafting general content
- Brainstorming
- Summarizing non-sensitive information
- Writing or improving code where approved
- Research using publicly available information
- Other approved business activities

## Information That Must Not Be Submitted to Unapproved AI Tools

Employees must not submit the following information to unapproved AI tools:

- Research participant personal information
- Employee personal information
- Passwords or credentials
- API keys or access tokens
- Confidential business information
- Financial information
- Sensitive research information
- Private client or partner information
- Information subject to contractual or legal restrictions

## Before Using an AI Tool

Employees should ask:

1. Is this AI tool approved by the organization?
2. What information am I about to share?
3. Does the information contain personal or confidential data?
4. Does the use comply with organizational policies?
5. Am I authorized to share this information?
6. Could the information create harm if exposed?

If the answer is unclear, the information should not be submitted until guidance is obtained.

## Security Requirements

Approved AI tools should be reviewed for:

- Data protection practices
- Data retention
- Access controls
- Authentication
- Vendor security
- Data processing arrangements
- Organizational requirements

## Incident Reporting

Employees must report suspected AI-related data exposure or accidental disclosure as soon as possible.

Examples include:

- Uploading sensitive information to an unapproved AI tool
- Sharing confidential information with an AI service
- Exposing credentials through an AI prompt
- Using an unapproved AI tool to process sensitive research data

## Enforcement

Failure to follow this policy may result in additional security review and appropriate organizational action in accordance with applicable company policies.

## Policy Review

This policy should be reviewed periodically and updated as AI tools, organizational risks, and applicable requirements change.

## Key Principle

**If you are not sure whether information is safe to share with an AI tool, do not share it until you have confirmed that the use is approved.**
