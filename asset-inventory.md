# Asset Inventory

## Purpose

The purpose of this inventory is to identify the key systems, information, and resources that Mwangaza Research needs to protect.

Understanding what the organization owns and what information it handles is the first step in identifying information security risks.

## Asset Inventory

| ID | Asset | Owner | Information / Function | Sensitivity | Location |
|---|---|---|---|---|---|
| A001 | Research Database | Research Team | Research participant information | Critical | Cloud |
| A002 | Employee Devices | IT | Business and employee information | High | Remote / Office |
| A003 | Google Workspace | IT | Email, documents and business information | High | Cloud |
| A004 | GitHub | Engineering | Source code and development information | High | Cloud |
| A005 | HR System | HR | Employee personal information | Critical | Cloud |
| A006 | Finance System | Finance | Financial and payment information | Critical | Cloud |
| A007 | AI Tools | IT / Security | Business information processed by AI tools | High | External Cloud |
| A008 | Vendor Accounts | IT / Procurement | Third-party services and business data | High | Cloud |

## Key Observations

The inventory identifies several high-value assets that require appropriate security controls.

The most sensitive assets are:

- Research participant information
- Employee personal information
- Financial information
- Source code
- Business information processed through AI tools

AI tools require particular attention because employees may unintentionally submit sensitive organizational or personal information to external services.

## Security Considerations

The following areas require further assessment:

1. Who has access to each asset?
2. Is access limited according to job responsibilities?
3. Is sensitive information encrypted?
4. How is access monitored?
5. How long is information retained?
6. Which third-party services can access organizational information?
7. What controls exist for the use of AI tools?

## Next Step

The identified assets will be used to develop the organization's information security risk register.
