# AI Tool Security Assessment

## Purpose

The purpose of this assessment is to evaluate the security and data protection risks associated with the use of AI tools by Mwangaza Research employees.

The assessment focuses on what information may be entered into an AI tool, how sensitive that information is, and what controls should be in place before the tool is approved for organizational use.

## Assessment Criteria

Each AI tool is assessed against the following areas:

- Type of information processed
- Data sensitivity
- Privacy risk
- Security risk
- Organizational use
- Required controls
- Overall decision

## AI Tool Assessment

| Tool | Intended Use | Data That May Be Entered | Privacy Risk | Security Risk | Overall Risk | Decision |
|---|---|---|---|---|---|---|
| Public AI Chatbot | General writing and research | Public information only | Medium | Medium | Medium | Restricted |
| Enterprise AI Assistant | Internal productivity | Approved business information | Medium | Medium | Medium | Conditional |
| AI Coding Assistant | Software development | Source code | Medium | High | High | Conditional |
| AI Data Analysis Tool | Data analysis | Research datasets | High | High | Critical | Restricted |
| Internal Approved AI Tool | Internal analysis | Approved organizational data | Low | Medium | Medium | Allowed with controls |

## Key Finding

The main risk is not simply whether an AI tool is secure.

The organization must also consider **what information employees are allowed to put into the tool**.

A tool may be useful for productivity while still creating unacceptable risk if employees submit personal, confidential, or sensitive information.

## High-Risk Example

### Scenario

A researcher uploads a spreadsheet containing research participant information into a public AI tool to summarize the data.

### Security Concerns

Potential concerns include:

- Exposure of personal information
- Loss of confidentiality
- Unclear data retention
- Third-party processing
- Unauthorized access
- Lack of organizational control over the information

### Risk Decision

**Restricted**

Research participant information should not be submitted to the tool unless the organization has assessed and approved the specific use case and appropriate safeguards are in place.

## Recommended Controls

The organization should consider:

1. Approved AI tool list
2. AI acceptable-use policy
3. Data classification rules
4. Employee security awareness
5. Access controls
6. Vendor due diligence
7. Data protection assessment
8. Incident reporting process
9. Periodic review of approved AI tools

## Assessment Outcome

AI tools should be evaluated based on both:

**The security of the tool**

and

**The sensitivity of the information being processed.**

AI use should therefore be managed using a risk-based approach rather than allowing unrestricted use of public AI tools.

## Next Step

The identified risks will be translated into an AI acceptable-use policy for employees.
