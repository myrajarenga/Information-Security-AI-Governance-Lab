# Information Security Risk Register

## Purpose

The purpose of this risk register is to identify, assess, and prioritize information security risks affecting Mwangaza Research.

The assessment considers the likelihood of a risk occurring and the potential impact if the risk occurs.

## Risk Rating

| Rating | Meaning |
|---|---|
| Low | Limited impact and/or unlikely to occur |
| Medium | Moderate impact or likelihood |
| High | Significant impact and/or likely to occur |
| Critical | Severe impact and/or highly likely to occur |

## Risk Register

| ID | Risk | Affected Asset | Likelihood | Impact | Risk Level | Proposed Control |
|---|---|---|---|---|---|---|
| R001 | Employee submits research participant information to an unapproved AI tool | Research Database / AI Tools | High | Critical | Critical | AI acceptable-use policy and employee awareness |
| R002 | Employee account is compromised through phishing | Employee Devices / Google Workspace | High | High | High | MFA and security awareness training |
| R003 | Former employee retains access to organizational systems | Google Workspace / GitHub | Medium | High | High | Joiner, mover and leaver access process |
| R004 | Excessive user permissions expose sensitive information | Research Database / HR System | Medium | Critical | High | Least privilege and regular access reviews |
| R005 | Third-party vendor mishandles organizational data | Vendor Accounts | Medium | High | High | Vendor security assessment |
| R006 | Sensitive information is stored without appropriate protection | Research Database / HR System | Medium | Critical | High | Encryption and data protection controls |
| R007 | Source code is exposed through a compromised developer account | GitHub | Medium | High | High | MFA, access controls and repository security |
| R008 | Security incident is not detected or escalated quickly | All critical assets | Medium | High | High | Monitoring and incident response procedures |

## Key Risk

The highest-priority scenario identified in this assessment is:

**R001: Employee submits research participant information to an unapproved AI tool.**

This risk combines information security, data protection, and AI governance concerns.

Potential consequences include:

- Unauthorized disclosure of personal information
- Loss of confidentiality
- Regulatory or contractual consequences
- Reputational damage
- Loss of trust from research participants or partners

## Risk Treatment

The proposed treatment for R001 is to reduce the risk through a combination of:

1. AI acceptable-use rules
2. Employee security awareness
3. Approved AI tool list
4. Data classification
5. Access controls
6. Incident reporting procedures
7. Periodic review of AI tools

## Next Step

The identified AI-related risk will be examined in greater detail through an AI tool security and risk assessment.
